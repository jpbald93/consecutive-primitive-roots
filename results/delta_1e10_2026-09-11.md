# δ(10¹⁰): a third data point for the Persistence Conjecture — 2026-09-11

## Why this was run

The manuscript (Conjecture, "Persistence") says:

> The measurements δ(10⁸) = −0.01336 and δ(10⁹) = −0.01414 do not determine
> whether δ(x) → 0 as x → ∞ or tends to a small nonzero limit, and we do not
> claim a prediction either way.

Two points cannot distinguish decay from convergence. This adds a third.

## Result

Full independent census to 10¹⁰ using `code/independent_audit_1e9.c` (the
disjoint implementation: no GMP, `__int128` modular exponentiation, `p−1`
factored by a segmented sieve of factors), run as 96 shards on jack
(32 cores), stitched with `code/stitch_audit.py` logic including the 95
shard-boundary pairs.

```
n_primes  = 455,052,508        (π(10¹⁰) = 455,052,511, minus p = 2, 3, 5)
n_artin   = 170,169,241
density   = 0.373955           (Artin's constant 0.3739558 — agrees to 6 dp)
n_pairs   = 455,052,507
table     = [[176781170, 108102097], [108102097, 62067143]]
delta     = -0.014723292716
chi2      = 98644.165750
gaps 20/60: 20,700,958 pairs, 0 doubly-Artin
```

The prime count is a strong independent check: it lands exactly on π(10¹⁰)
minus the three excluded primes, and the Artin density reproduces Artin's
constant to six decimal places over 455 million primes.

**The Paper 1 theorem holds at 10¹⁰**: zero doubly-Artin pairs among all
20,700,958 consecutive pairs with gap 20 or 60 — a ninefold larger test set
than the 2,195,882 pairs available at 10⁹.

## What the three points show

| x | δ(x) | change |
|---|---|---|
| 10⁸ | −0.013360 | — |
| 10⁹ | −0.014140 | −0.000780 |
| 10¹⁰ | −0.014723 | −0.000583 |

Two things are now visible that were not with two points:

1. **δ is not decaying toward 0.** It is growing *more* negative with x. The
   "δ(x) → 0" branch the manuscript raises as a caution is not what the data
   do over this range.
2. **The increments are shrinking** (0.000780 → 0.000583, ratio ≈ 0.75),
   which is what convergence to a small nonzero limit looks like. Taking the
   increments as geometric with that ratio extrapolates to roughly −0.0165,
   but see the caveat below.

This is evidence *for* the Persistence Conjecture as stated (δ negative for
all sufficiently large x) and *against* the separable-channel heuristic's
δ → 0 suggestion — at least over 10⁸–10¹⁰.

## Caveat, stated plainly

Three points, no error bars on the asymptotics, and a geometric-increment
extrapolation is an assumption rather than a result. Nothing here is a proof
of a nonzero limit, and the separable-channel argument in the manuscript is
not refuted — it concerns a limit that these finite measurements cannot
reach. The honest claim is: *over three decades the correlation strengthens
with decelerating increments, which is inconsistent with the simplest decay
picture.*

Recommended use in the manuscript: report δ(10¹⁰) alongside the other two and
note the direction and the deceleration. Do not assert a limit.
